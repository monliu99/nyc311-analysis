Data collection has been broadly consistent over the 4,342 days from Jan 1, 2010 to Nov 20, 2021, since every day has requests and the median day sees 6,512 openings, but there are three breaks the Deputy Mayor should know about. Twenty-four days are likely incomplete, mostly because batch files are missing (four days in January 2010 and 20 between September and November 2021), several recording practices changed partway through, and closing dates are less reliable than opening dates. Most of the spikes and dips in daily volume, on the other hand, are holidays and real events rather than collection failures.

Of the 148 unusually low days, 78 are federal holidays, and the high days tend to have a clear cause in the complaint mix; on Aug 4, 2020, for example, 311 logged 24,415 requests against a usual 9,548 for that weekday, with 97% of the excess being Damaged Tree complaints. The changes in collection itself are quieter, as several agencies (DOF, HRA, NYCEM and a few smaller ones) disappear from the data in June 2019, HPD recorded opening dates without a time of day until 2015 (98.9% of its requests in 2010, 0.1% by 2016), and 422,505 older requests, mostly DOB's, were loaded into later files.

Regarding closing times, 98.0% of requests have a close date, but 261,538 were closed before they were opened (7.53% of DOT's closed requests), 1,044,470 were closed at the very second they were opened, which likely reflects administrative closures rather than work done, and 5,801 carry a 1900-01-01 placeholder. On 134 days an agency closed at least five times its usual volume; on Sep 10, 2019, for example, DSNY closed 18,470 requests against a usual 811, 95% of them at the same midnight timestamp, which looks more like a backlog clean-up than a day's work (Q4 shows how these records affect response times).

The second chart below is built for the city's own presentations and shows average openings and closings per day by weekday and week of the year, over the full years 2010 to 2020. Openings peak on Tuesdays (7,120 a day) and closings on Thursdays (7,200), and both fall on weekends, closings more so (4,002 on Sundays against 4,849 openings), which means that agencies fall behind over the weekend and catch up later in the week. Across the year the average is fairly flat, apart from a summer high in week 25 (late June, 7,202 a day) and the holiday low in week 52 (5,284), although single years, 2020 above all, can stray well above it. Both views show when requests are logged and closed, which is not necessarily when problems occur.
